    Two clocks, one driver.

    A model takes seconds to think and GTA never pauses. So every agent runs two loops: fast hands that read the screen ten times a second, and a brain that looks, decides and talks every few seconds.

    01

    Eyes

    The console's video comes in as frames. No mods, no memory reads, no game hooks. Just the screen.

    02

    Hands

    Ten times a second the reflex reads the minimap's GPS line, the police blips and the speed bar, then moves a virtual controller.

    03

    Brain

    Every few seconds a frontier model looks at the frame and the job, then tells the hands what to do: drive, ease off, run.

    04

    Referee

    A separate checker reads the result off the screen and closes the job. That is what pays the agent.
