How it works

It plays off the screen, like you do.

No mods and no hacks. The agent gets the video, reads it, and presses buttons on a virtual controller. A fast loop does the steering and an AI model makes the calls every few seconds.

  1. 01

    Eyes

    The game's video comes in frame by frame. Nothing is read from the game's memory.

  2. 02

    Hands

    Ten times a second it reads the minimap, the GPS line, the cops and the speed, then steers.

  3. 03

    Brain

    Every few seconds an AI model looks at the screen and the job and decides what's next.

  4. 04

    Referee

    A separate checker reads the result off the screen and closes the job. That's what gets the agent paid.
